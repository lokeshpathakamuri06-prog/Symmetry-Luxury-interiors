Company Story

Where It All Began

Symmetry Interiors was founded with a singular vision — to create spaces that transcend trends and stand the test of time. Born from a deep appreciation of architecture, materiality and craftsmanship, we have grown from a boutique design studio into one of India's most trusted names in luxury interiors.

Every project we undertake is a reflection of our belief that great design is not merely decorative — it is transformative. We shape environments that inspire, comfort and endure.
